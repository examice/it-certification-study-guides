# IT certification study guides and practice questions

Free study guides for 10 IT certification exams from Microsoft, GitHub, AWS,
CompTIA, and Anthropic. Each guide summarizes the official exam outline and
adds a study plan, a cheat sheet, the free official resources, and 15
original practice questions with explanations. Below the guides: where to
practice for free, and what the paid practice sites cost.

> Unofficial study notes by [Examice](https://examice.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=it-certification-study-guides&utm_content=link_1).
> Not affiliated with or endorsed by any exam vendor. The practice questions
> are original and not taken from any exam.

## How the guides are written

- Every exam fact (domains and weights, duration, passing score, price,
  renewal, retirement dates) comes from the vendor's official page, linked
  with the date it was checked.
- Every practice question is written new for its guide, with an explanation
  that links to the vendor's documentation. None is taken from an exam.
- When a vendor announces a new version of an exam, the guide says so at the
  top until it is updated.
